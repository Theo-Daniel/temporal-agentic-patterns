How it works

Three mechanisms do the real work — and they are ordered, not competing.

Self-healing

The model sees its own mistake

A retry here is not a blind retry. The workflow validates the model's output, then sends the bad response and the specific validation error back as real conversation turns, so the next attempt can see exactly what it got wrong. Bounded at three attempts — no infinite loops.

Update

A correction that answers back

When extraction runs out of attempts, the employee supplies the real amount through a Temporal Update — a synchronous, validated call. A bad value is rejected with the reason on the spot; a good one returns accepted and the workflow carries on where it stopped.

Signal

A human gate that costs nothing to wait on

Anything over policy — or classified as anything but low risk — parks on a Temporal Signal and waits. The manager's approve or reject is fire-and-forget, and the workflow's state is durable the whole time it waits, whether that's five seconds or five days.

Built for what happens after launch

Three more mechanisms — for the parts of running a workflow forever that a happy-path demo usually skips.

Versioning

Old workflows don't replay the new prompt

The extraction prompt changed mid-project — twice. workflow.patched() is called once, at the top of the workflow, so a claim already in flight when the prompt changes keeps replaying against the prompt it actually ran, while every new claim gets the current one — same code, same deploy, no non-deterministic replay error.

Heartbeat

A crash doesn't cost the tokens already spent

Risk classification heartbeats its generated text roughly once a second. If the worker dies mid-generation, Temporal's retry reads the last heartbeat and prompts the model to continue from there instead of starting over — a prompted continuation, not a true token-level resume, and documented as exactly that rather than oversold.

Continue-As-New

History that resets itself before it becomes a problem

The orchestrator watches its own event-history length after each stage. Past a threshold, it snapshots its progress to an external store and calls continue_as_new — same workflow ID, fresh history, invisible to anything watching from outside.
